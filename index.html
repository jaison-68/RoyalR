How the model works

Built only from the supplied ball-by-ball data. The full method, checks and limitations are in the written report.

Score every ball

A win-probability model (AUC 0.86) estimates how much each ball can swing the match. In the first innings, pressure means a collapse, the death overs or a knockout.

Set an expectation

For each ball, models predict the runs and wickets an average player gets in that exact spot, so a rebuild at 40 for 4 is not judged like the 19th over.

Rate fairly

Small samples are pulled back toward average. Recent seasons count more, and runs or wickets against Associate teams count half.

Stress-test it

The whole ranking is re-run on 300 reshuffles of the matches and five different weightings. Players who keep their place rank higher.
